# Indonesian NIK and KTP: A Helpful Guide

## What Is NIK in Indonesia?

NIK stands for Nomor Induk Kependudukan. It is a unique 16-digit identification number assigned to Indonesian residents.

NIK is used for population administration, identity verification, and various public services in Indonesia.

## What Is a KTP?

KTP (Kartu Tanda Penduduk) is the Indonesian national identity card. It contains important personal identification information, including the holder's NIK.

## Where Can You Find Your NIK Number?

You can generally find your NIK in these documents:

1. Indonesian electronic identity card (e-KTP).
2. Kartu Keluarga (Family Card).
3. Official population administration records.

## How Can You Verify Your NIK?

If you need to verify your NIK, contact your local Dukcapil office or use the official verification services available in your area.

Always protect your personal identification information. Avoid sharing your NIK on unknown websites.
